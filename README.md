# 🥗 NutriAdvisor

**🌟 Try the Live Demo:** [Click here to test NutriAdvisor!](https://nutriadvisor.vercel.app/)

NutriAdvisor is a full-stack application designed to provide intelligent, personalized nutritional analysis from food imagery. 

By leveraging Google's Gemini multimodal AI, the application accepts an image of a meal alongside specific user health goals (such as "muscle gain" or "weight loss") and dietary restrictions (like "vegan" or "gluten-free"). 

It processes this information to return a comprehensive, structured breakdown that includes:
* **Food Detection:** Identification of individual items on the plate.
* **Nutritional Estimation:** Calculated estimates for total calories and macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat, fiber).
* **Goal-Oriented Scoring:** A health score from 1-10 evaluating how well the meal aligns with the user's specific objectives.
* **Actionable Advice:** Tailored recommendations and healthier ingredient swaps based on the provided dietary profile.

Ultimately, NutriAdvisor serves as an intelligent dietary assistant, turning simple photos into structured, actionable health data.
